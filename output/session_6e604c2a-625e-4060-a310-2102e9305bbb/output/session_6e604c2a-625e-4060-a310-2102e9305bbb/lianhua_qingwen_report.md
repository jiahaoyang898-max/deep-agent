### 2.2 Formula Characteristics

The formula embodies four treatment principles: **"Clear, Disperse, Drain, Unblock"**

- **Clear:** Multiple heat-clearing and detoxifying herbs (Lianqiao, Jinyinhua, Banlangen, Yu Xing Cao) work together to eliminate heat toxins;
- **Disperse:** Zhi Ma Huang disseminates lung Qi, providing pathways for pathogens to exit;
- **Drain:** Shi Gao clears excess heat from lungs and stomach; Da Huang purges heat through bowels;
- **Unblock:** Hong Jing Tian invigorates qi and blood circulation; Guang Huo Xiang resolves dampness and harmonizes spleen and stomach.

## 3. Therapeutic Effects

### 3.1 Indications

**Core Action:** Clear plague toxins, detoxify, disseminate lung, and purge heat.

**TCM Syndrome Pattern:** Heat-toxin attacking the lung syndrome.

### 3.2 Target Symptoms

Lianhua Qingwen Capsules are suitable for patients presenting the following symptoms:

| Symptom Category | Manifestations |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Fever-related | Fever or high fever, chills, muscle soreness |
| Nasal symptoms | Nasal congestion, runny nose |
| Respiratory symptoms | Cough, thick yellow phlegm |
| Head/facial symptoms | Headache, dry throat, sore throat |
| Tongue/pulse signs | Red tongue, yellow or greasy yellow coating |

### 3.3 Applicable vs. Non-applicable Users

**APPROPRIATE FOR:**
- Patients diagnosed with "Heat-toxin attacking lung syndrome" in TCM;
- Those with predominant high fever, severe headache, marked throat pain, and thick yellow sputum;
- People falling under wind-heat cold or heat syndrome patterns.

**NOT appropriate for:**
- Wind-cold cold sufferers with heavy chills, mild fever, no sweating, clear nasal discharge, white thin phlegm;
- Cold constitution individuals who always feel cold and have loose stools;
- Healthy asymptomatic persons: Lianhua Qingwen is for treatment, not prevention.

### 4.2 Anti-inflammatory and Immune-modulating Effects

- Suppresses cytokine storm by significantly reducing TNF-alpha, IL-6, MCP-1, IP-10 and other pro-inflammatory cytokines;
- Regulates signaling pathways including MAPK1, NLRP3, HSP90AA1, TLR9;
- Protects lung tissue by reducing pulmonary inflammatory lesions and improving lung function;
- Enhances immunity by increasing CD4+ T lymphocyte count and CD4+/CD8+ ratio, promoting interferon-gamma secretion.

- **Antitussive and Expectorant:** Promotes tracheal phenol red secretion, prolongs cough latency, improves airway secretion expulsion.

### 4.4 Antibacterial Activity

Shows significant inhibitory effect on Staphylococcus aureus including methicillin-resistant S. aureus (MRSA), helping prevent secondary bacterial infections.

| Other | Dry mouth, dizziness | Generally mild, may diminish with continued use |

### 7.2 Contraindications

Contraindicated in persons allergic to any component of this product.

### 7.3 Precautions

1. Avoid tobacco, alcohol, spicy, raw, cold and greasy foods during treatment;
2. Do NOT take simultaneously with tonic Chinese herbal medicines such as Ginseng or Astragalus;
3. Avoid concurrent use with other heat-clearing drugs to prevent worsening diarrhea;
4. Not applicable to wind-cold cold patients;
5. Use with caution in hypertension and heart disease patients because it contains Ephedra which may raise blood pressure;
6. Chronic disease patients including liver disease, diabetes, kidney disease should use under physician guidance;
7. Children, pregnant or lactating women, elderly or frail persons should use under physician guidance;
8. Patients with fever over 38.5 degrees Celsius should seek hospital care rather than relying solely on this medication;
9. Strictly follow prescribed dosage, do not use long-term, seek medical help if no improvement after 3 days;
10. Allergy-prone individuals should use cautiously, athletes should use cautiously because Ephedra may trigger positive doping tests;
11. Contains Ku Xing Ren and Guan Zhong, both mildly toxic, never exceed maximum specified dosage.
